A little about us

We are parents, living the career life, who find fun and joy in all things cute, whimsical, and, of course, Nerdy. We began 3D printing just for fun, and promptly dove into the deep end. The discovery of new items, cleaning them up, and painting them quickly became our favorite hobby. Now, we want to share some of that fun with you!

What do we make?

Desk Pals, Display Items, Hand-Painted Figurines, Fidget Toys, Mini Fidget Pets, Dashboard Buddies, Pocket Worlds, and Key Chains. These are only some items we currently have in inventory. But the possibilities are vast!

What do we do?

We are printers. While graphic design and modeling is a dream of ours, it is not a skill learned overnight. There are many items we have already purchased the license to print and sell. Please, look around. If what you want isn't there, reach out and we will try to find it.

Customizations

We are able to do basic customizations. For example, if we have a brown dog and you want it to be black, we can do so. We are also able to make size adjustments, though there are limits depending on the complexities and articulations of the model. While there are many options out there, not all filament is suitable for all printers, projects, and environments. We have chosen the filament and brands that we have found work best with our machine, prints, and most importantly, our safety. Our filament brand of choice is eSun PLA Plus for its strength and range of colors.
