Filament Selection

PLA: best for non-functional prints. Matte looks the best, but is slightly weaker. Polymaker is our supplier of choice.
PETG: better for functional or outdoor prints, but is trickier to print than PLA.
TPU: flexible and the hardest to print, so take your time calibrating your filament.
Advanced Filaments: many of these filaments emit toxic gases when printed (i.e., ABS), so make sure you follow the appropriate safety guidelines.

Printer Calibration

Your printer will prompt you to run the normal calibrations and you should recalibrate whenever you move it to a new location.
However, to get the best prints possible you also need to calibrate your printer settings for your specific filament. To do this, follow this tutorial: Calibration Masterclass
