Printer Calibration

Your printer will prompt you to run the normal calibrations, which you should always do. Also recalibrate your printer whenever you move it to a new location. However, to get the best prints possible you also need to calibrate your printer settings for your specific filament. To do this, follow this tutorial: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gVU5If1VsAM
